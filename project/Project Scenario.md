Scenario C — Community Sports & Activity Booking System
A community organisation manages facilities such as sports pitches, sports halls,
courts and other activity spaces.
Bookings are currently managed using a combination of phone calls, email,
spreadsheets and calendars.
The organisation is considering a software system to improve the booking process.
Current problems include:
• conflicting bookings
• difficulty checking availability
• cancellations and no-shows
• staff spending time answering booking queries
• difficulty keeping information up to date
• different facilities having different rules
• the need to protect user information
• accessibility and usability concerns
As the Requirements Engineer, investigate questions such as:
• Who can make a booking?
• Can individuals and groups make bookings?
• What information is required?
• How far in advance can facilities be booked?
• Are different facilities subject to different rules?
• What happens when a booking is cancelled?
• What happens when someone does not attend?
• Can staff override a booking?
• Who can see booking information?
• What privacy and security requirements apply?
• What accessibility requirements should be considered?
Important: These questions are prompts to help you begin your investigation. They are
not a list of requirements to copy into your project. As your project develops, identify
additional stakeholders, questions, needs, constraints and exceptions.
